# Changelog

All notable changes to Open Fantacalcio Manager are documented here.
Format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/) and adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-08

### 🚀 Features
- **Cross-Platform Support**: macOS, Windows, Linux, Android, and iOS builds.
- **Offline Persistence**: SQLite database preserving all live auction state, notes, and rosters.
- **Smart Spreadsheet Importer**: Header-based `.xlsx` and `.csv` parsing with automatic banner detection and Ceduti filtering.
- **Master Strategy Board ("Listone")**: Instant `Valore Base Asta` computation from `% Budget`, configurable tiers, favorites, and FVM bulk normalization.
- **Per-Role Strategy Tabs**: Focused views for Goalkeepers, Defenders, Midfielders, and Forwards with inline target prices and notes.
- **Live Auction Mode**: Search and autocomplete, persistent sticky mini-dashboard, and 1-tap actions: *Assegna a me*, *Venduto ad altri*, and *Rimetti disponibile*.
- **La Mia Rosa**: Real-time budget tracking (Allocato / Speso / Residuo) with visual usage meters and roster management.
- **Export Engine**: Export LISTONE and Squadra back to `.xlsx` and `.csv`.
- **Bilingual & Dual Theme**: Italian and English support with Light and Dark themes.
- **Repository Hooks**: Conventional Commits enforcement and automated semantic versioning.
